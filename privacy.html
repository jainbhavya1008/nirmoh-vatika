Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Information this website collects

Nirmoh Vatika is a static website. It has no accounts, comments, contact form, newsletter, payment system, or server-side database, and it does not ask visitors to submit personal information.

Files and third parties

Book PDFs are delivered from this website. Font files are requested from Google Fonts when the site loads; Google may process technical request data under its own privacy policy. The hosting provider may also keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and time of request.

Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is not active by default. If the site owner enables it in a future deployment, it will load only after a visitor chooses “Accept analytics”; that choice is stored in the browser. This policy must be updated before analytics is enabled.

Your choices

You can decline analytics if the consent notice is shown, or clear your browser storage to remove a previous choice. For questions about this policy, please use the contact method published by the site owner.
